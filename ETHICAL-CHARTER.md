# Ethical Design and Mathematical Integrity

**Salīre’s Ethical Charter — Draft 1**

Salīre is developed with respect for intellectual autonomy, psychological well-being, mathematical integrity and the individual's right to independent discovery.

Every puzzle is intended to contain exactly one mathematically valid solution, achievable through its disclosed arithmetic and logical constraints, without requiring guessing, undisclosed rules or predetermined reasoning methods.

Difficulty is intended to stimulate intellectual growth, not manufacture failure, undermine self-confidence, exploit psychological vulnerabilities or create unnecessary cognitive burdens.

All assistance shall remain voluntary, player-initiated and designed to support independent reasoning without diminishing the accomplishment of discovery.

Salīre shall not employ manipulative engagement mechanisms, behavioral exploitation, undisclosed performance monitoring or artificial barriers to progression.

Puzzle performance is not a measure of intelligence, personal worth or intellectual potential. Players are encouraged to progress at their own pace, according to their individual interests, abilities and aspirations.

The educational and psychological benefits described are design objectives, not guarantees of individual outcomes.

**The integrity of the player's intellectual experience shall take precedence over engagement, retention, completion rates and commercial interests.**
