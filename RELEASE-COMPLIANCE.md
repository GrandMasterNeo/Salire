# Release compliance gate

Do not distribute imported code or binaries until the exact upstream license and notices are preserved, modification notices are complete, corresponding source obligations are satisfied, dependency and asset rights are checked, and privacy disclosures are verified. Check Salīre trademark clearance before public branding; the ™ symbol does not establish registration. Preserve Git history and attribution. No additional restrictions may be imposed on GPL-covered software. This checklist is not legal advice or a certification of compliance.
